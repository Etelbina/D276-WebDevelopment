Career Goals

Building a Career with Purpose

Choosing to transition from music to software engineering was one of the most significant decisions of my professional life. It meant leaving a field I deeply valued to pursue a new challenge that better aligned with my curiosity, interest in logical problem solving, and desire for continuous growth.

My goal is to build a long-term career as a software engineer by developing both technical expertise and professional experience. I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering at Western Governors University, where every course, project, and new challenge brings me one step closer to becoming the kind of engineer I aspire to be.

Short-Term Goals

  • Complete my B.S. in Software Engineering within approximately two years.
  • Continue expanding my portfolio with projects demonstrating software development skills.
  • Secure software engineering internship in collaborative development.
  • Develop workflow for AI-assisted tools, learning to use them responsibly while strengthening problem-solving skills.

Mid-Term Goals

  • Begin my career as a software engineer.
  • Contribute to production software used by real users.
  • Expand my knowledge of software architecture, testing, and scalable application design.
  • Become a reliable contributor within collaborative engineering teams while learning from experienced developers.

Long-Term Goals

I hope to build a sustainable career that allows me to take on meaningful technical challenges while maintaining the balance needed to pursue my interests, relationships, and personal well-being.

I believe long-term success comes not only from technical excellence, but also from curiosity, consistency, and a healthy approach to both work and life.

Looking Ahead

As my career progresses, I hope to contribute to increasingly complex software projects involving larger codebases, multidisciplinary teams, and greater technical responsibility.

I also look forward to mentoring others as I gain experience and contributing to products that make a meaningful difference for the people who use them.
